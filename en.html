Rocket Translator User Guide

Rocket Translator is a lightweight free online translation website. No software download or account registration required, just open the webpage to use. It supports bidirectional translation between Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean. Click the Swap Languages button to quickly switch translation directions without manual selection. You can translate single vocabulary, daily sentences, essays and long novel paragraphs rapidly. Ideal for students finishing homework, professionals reading foreign documents, and daily overseas communication.

Official website fanyi.xzakeji.icu, fully encrypted via HTTPS protocol, fast page loading speed with stable access from overseas users. Press Enter key to trigger translation quickly, one-click copy of translated text, simple and easy operation.

Website Statement: Rocket Translator only provides free online text translation tools. This website is hosted on overseas servers and does not hold domestic ICP filing. We only offer text translation services and will never store users' private content. Contact the webmaster via email nameiszcy@126.com if you have any questions or content issues.
